# Measured scale, cover framing and readable thoughts

These rules supersede independently fitted pose sizes, tiny opening-page motifs, and the former fixed 650px thought-cloud width. Apply them to all 442 page slots. Read each page's `geometry_continuity` contract and its chapter's `camera-continuity.json` before generating references or compositing. Planning coordinates remain proposals until actual returned pixels are measured.

## Same location, camera and depth means the same scale

Record a camera group using location/background identity, canvas, background transform, framing/zoom, horizon/ground and subject depth. Pages sharing that group must share physical scale. A changed expression, hand gesture or pose does not authorize a different zoom. A different camera or intentional close-up must be explicitly recorded; do not infer a zoom to excuse a mismatch. Opening close-ups and editorial diagrams may have their own declared camera while keeping the book's outer frame.

Choose one checked baseline for each recurring person, chair and prop in that camera group. Save actual native landmark endpoints, target pixel distances, source hash, ground/seat anchors and chosen uniform scale. For a character, use at least two useful landmarks: for example crown-to-chin plus shoulder width or a stable torso length; for equivalent upright seated poses, crown-to-ground is also useful. Hair volume, a bent knee, a raised arm or a tilted head can alter the bounding box without changing the character's scale. Do not use the entire sprite rectangle as the sole scale test.

Compute `uniform scale = target landmark distance / measured native landmark distance`. If several comparable landmarks are available, compare their proposed scales before selecting one. A sprite from a different generation usually needs a different resize percentage to reach the SAME final physical size. Do not copy a percentage from another source sheet. Never stretch a character in x/y, resize the head alone or resize a chair independently inside an interacting group.

At equal depth and camera, target a maximum 1.5% difference for directly comparable measured anatomical/prop distances; final support anchors should match within 3px when the pose has not moved. These are audit tolerances, not permission to erase pose geometry. Explain projected changes from head tilt, foreshortening or real movement with actual landmarks. If two invariant measurements demand incompatible scales, the source proportions are inconsistent: use a compatible accepted source or the permitted precise source correction. Do not hide the conflict by distorting anatomy or changing the room zoom.

Check one character's scale against the fixed room/table AND adjacent characters. Keep independently owned room, table and other actors unchanged when correcting one pose. Compare alternating pages at identical display zoom, then review the whole camera group as a contact sheet. Record numerical deltas plus the visual verdict. A pass on one page alone does not establish continuity.

Chapter 1's matched social seated-pilot group uses a 1233.18px painted crown-to-ground target and ground anchor y=1614.36. The conversing source uses 0.765; the quiet source requires about 0.79253, not its previous 0.77. Their crosschecked crown-to-chin sizes differ by under 1%. These numbers belong to those two measured sources and this camera; do not copy them into other poses or chapters.

## Covers use the same painted field and border

A chapter opening is a full illustration slot. Do not place a tiny isolated object in the centre of an otherwise white canvas. Match the chapter's checked full-scene painted footprint and paper-border treatment. Measure the painted area's `[left, top, right, bottom]`, normalized margins and occupied width/height; the canvas dimensions alone do not describe the border.

Use the same background transform as the adjacent full scene where appropriate. An editorial still life can use the shared blank watercolor cover field, without importing a location, character or unmentioned object. The opening's hero may be an explicit close-up; preserve its proportions and use its native pixel budget. A large supporting painted field supplies consistent framing without making a small flower or crown absurdly large relative to an in-world scene.

The initial 2K background target remains the existing 2000×1493 field near x200/y149; actual larger returns can use their native checked framing. Once the first full scene is measured, lock the entire chapter's border to that result, including its cover. Compare each side against that baseline; flag a difference over 2 percentage points of canvas width/height. Ragged deckled edges are judged by their substantial paint boundary, not a few isolated speckles. Do not silently force every scene into a new camera to fit a nominal rectangle.

Chapter 1's cover now uses a 2280×1768 supporting wash field near x60/y24, measured by its actual painted boundary rather than transparent padding. Its paper, pencil and canonical drawing are kept at native size. Only the empty outer wood wash is extended; no object is stretched. This is an explicit nonfigurative-background exception, not permission to stretch scenery, furniture, characters or drawings. Inspect the seam/texture and edge spill before accepting any extension.

Future editorial covers already have a shared blank-field request in their dependency lists. Generate it once, audit it, and reuse it; do not spend a separate generation per cover. Full-location opening plates retain their existing scene backgrounds. Updated close-up envelopes are upper bounds; the actual hero must remain readable and at scale ≤1. If native pixels cannot support a planned cover, resolve that source during preflight rather than exporting a tiny fallback.

## Size the thought image first, the cloud second

The requested minimalism concerns the decorative cloud and its empty padding, not the picture inside it. First define a readable AND visually subordinate size for the actual thought content using its meaningful painted bounds. Preserve the original image's aspect ratio and pigment strength. Never reduce the content merely to obey a fixed frame width; an oversized simple motif should be resized deliberately when it competes with the action, without making its meaning unreadable. For current Chapter 1 the sleeping-snake picture is 640px wide and Drawing Two is 650px wide, independently of the surrounding frame.

Fit a restrained painted frame around that content. Aim for roughly 78–85% content width within the frame and a small clear breathing space; adapt for irregular silhouettes. Keep the picture's significant pigment entirely inside the safe interior. A zero-clipping pixel check and reading-size inspection are both required. Do not use a bounding box full of blank source paper as the content-size measurement.

The registered large painted puff can serve as a simple frame: only this EMPTY decorative artwork may be adapted independently in x/y to match the content's shape. The thought image itself is uniformly resized from its original source, never warped, cropped, sharpened, redrawn or faded. Record this frame-only resampling explicitly. Use an opaque cream interior so room lines cannot show through the thought picture, with a soft painted outer edge and three small trail puffs pointing to the actual thinker. No white rectangular insert, thick synthetic outline or arbitrary blurry cream patch.

If an existing frame cannot fit the readable image gracefully, use a specifically prepared blank-frame generation with the required interior aspect/padding and the existing style reference, subject to the same credit and three-attempt rules. Do not quietly shrink the thought image. Try a compatible accepted blank painted component first; Chapter 1 required no new generation.

## Thought aesthetics and whole-page balance

The story action and faces are primary; the thought is a quiet supporting image. Matching watercolor paper alone does not establish a style match. Match the characters' simplified shape language, thin warm contours, broad gentle washes and detail density. Ordinary hat thoughts should not resemble detailed product paintings. A mental map uses a few generalized landforms and a pale water wash, with no fine coastlines, terrain partitions, grids, labels, political borders, route or realistic folds. Canonical Drawing One/Two pigments remain exact.

Choose content size by recognizable meaning and visual balance at the actual reading size, then fit the minimal empty frame. Do not apply a universal 640px minimum: the approved complex snake/elephant pictures retain their specific 640/650px widths, while a simple hat can be about 330–380px and a simple map about 420–480px on Chapter 1's 2400px canvas. These are starting measurements for these motifs, not book-wide quotas. Preserve aspect ratio and pigment; do not hide an overly detailed source by blurring or fading it. A large source sheet supplies clean native pixels, not permission for an oversized final inset.

Reserve quiet negative space before generating and composing. If the complete thought cannot sit gracefully, re-plan the whole page: move or uniformly resize complete interacting groups on an explicitly separate camera, leaving faces, hand contacts, orientation and border intact. Record the changed camera and all measurements. Within a locked same-camera/depth sequence, retain established physical scale and solve the layout coherently across that group. Never shrink only the pilot inside his plane or a person's head. The user specifically authorized recomposing Chapter 1's night-flight view; day and night are distinct opposite-facing views, not a fixed room camera.

At 800px page width, compare with the approved artwork: first read the action/faces, then the thought without strain. Check its visual weight, simplicity, location, border clearance, short owner-linked trail and the negative space separating it from the hero. Native inspection follows for extraction, paper and contours. A zero-overlap test alone does not prove an attractive composition. Record `thought_aesthetic_hierarchy` for every page: actual visual evidence when selected, or the concrete no-cloud reason. Local sizing/framing/layout repairs do not consume generation attempts; conspicuously incompatible rendering may use only the remaining justified attempt.
