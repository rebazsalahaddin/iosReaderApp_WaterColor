## Establish the shot before paying for sources

For each page record the exact text, one visible instant, actor and recipient, action, response, expression, prop state, thought decision and a meaningful visual difference from adjacent pages. Reusing a character or background is encouraged; copying the same finished scene under a new filename is forbidden. A changed filename, shadow opacity, grain or trivial translation does not create a distinct illustration. The difference must communicate the page: offer versus withdrawal, reply versus pleased agreement, a changed canonical drawing, a newly understood thought, or a motivated new view.

Create a shot list with establishing, medium interaction, useful detail and emotional emphasis where the passage benefits. Do not force a different camera on every page. Preserve screen direction and the line between speakers. A genuine angle change requires compatible complete character/contact-group and background perspectives. A local crop is a reframing, not a newly generated angle. Never relabel an accidental subject-size mismatch as a new camera.

Before generation, list camera ID, background hash and transform, horizon, floor/rug landmarks, depth of each actor, light direction, intended frame and source pixel budget. A camera change transforms the coherent scene, including every actor, prop, background and shadow; it does not selectively enlarge one character. Exclude nondiegetic thought frames from the scene camera and re-place them deliberately around the new framing. Keep the shared painted border. Any resampling of a background for a declared detail view must be recorded and checked; anatomical sources still cannot be secretly enlarged beyond native pixels.

## Measure people and props together

Create a persistent `scale-and-anchor-registry.json` per chapter and carry the same identity/prop ratios forward across chapters. Do not merely write “keep sizes consistent.” Store actual source hashes and native landmark endpoints, the authority image, target distances, uniform scale, source anchor and final anchor for EVERY placed component. Save at least two independent comparable measurements per recurring subject and at least three stable room/furniture landmarks per repeated camera.

Use `s = target_distance / native_distance`; place with `translation = target_anchor - s * native_anchor`. Crosscheck a second anatomical or rigid-prop measurement. A gesture changes the sprite bounding box, not the person's physical scale. A seated adult is not shrunk to a child's standing envelope; a sleeping person's bent length is not their standing height. Do not resize a head separately. Do not solve an internally wrong pilot/plane group by scaling the whole group and claiming its internal ratio was corrected.

At the same camera and depth, directly comparable anatomical/prop distances should agree within 1.5%; unchanged support anchors within 3px. Inspect real edges and report any source-shape/foreshortening uncertainty explicitly. Do not claim zero residuals by copying target numbers into evidence. If incompatible head/torso/chair measurements require different scales and the difference remains obvious, reject the complete source or use a compatible accepted state within the remaining attempt budget. No anatomical distortion.

Keep a fixed world/ground anchor for an actor who has not moved. Head, hands and lifted feet may move with a pose; chair-leg locations and stationary furniture should not. Record an actual story movement before moving the actor's support anchor. Check table-rim height, table-foot locations and chair seat against the room, not just the lowest alpha pixel. Check every adjacent pair at the same display zoom and camera-normalized scale. Check the whole chapter, including nonadjacent reuse.

## Grounding that touches the reference

Make one shadow owner for each complete supported group. Record individual bearing surfaces: each contacting shoe sole, chair/table-leg tip, wheel contact, seated hip or resting elbow. Different feet/wheels in perspective can touch different y positions. A floating object or imagined inset has no physical ground shadow. A lifted foot has no contact core.

New neutral masters include their own very light soft watercolor contact shadow on genuine transparency. Inspect every visible support on pale, dark and actual backgrounds: no gap, opaque floor, dark oval or detached wash. Expression variants preserve that exact shadow shape, position, hue, alpha, softness and intensity. Transform the complete group and its shadow together; do not add a programmatic shadow. Airborne assets and crops without a visible ground support have no invented floor shadow.

The retained production/compositor/grounding.py recipe applies only to accepted legacy sources whose current measured composition uses local grounding. It is not the default for new transparent sources. Preserve existing art and its measured recipes during prompt maintenance. At future composition, inspect actual bearing contacts after the final uniform transform and at reading size.
