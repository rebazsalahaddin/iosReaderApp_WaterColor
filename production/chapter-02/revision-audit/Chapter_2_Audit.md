# Chapter 2 v002 — actual image and construction audit

All 19 delivered pages were inspected against their passages, current layers, placement records, source variants and geometry artwork. Chapter 2 artwork was not changed. This is a diagnosis and correction handoff, not acceptance of v002. Chapter 1 v010 is the revised technique example, subject to the user's manual review.

## Findings and causes

1. **Pose envelopes substituted for physical scale.** c02-s02-p04 uses a pilot crop1669×1469 at scale0.530612, displayed886×780. The next page uses a different seated pilot at0.485362, displayed446×630, while the prince stays280×700 at0.458716. c02-s03-p03 fits the presenting pilot483×630 at0.475472 and leaning Prince415×700 at0.487126. The fact that a standing child is taller than a seated adult is not itself the defect: their independently measured heads/torso and canonical standing-equivalent ratios must agree. Here the visual head ratios do not. Bounding-box fit never verifies anatomy.
2. **Input authority conflict.** The supplied standing scale artwork has pilot crown-to-sole about1308, Prince800, head heights about286/245 (matching landmark definition). That gives Prince/pilot height≈0.612 and head≈0.86. The Prince turnaround is more chibi, with a larger head fraction. Generated Prince sources followed that identity image; this is not solely random model drift. Declare geometry artwork authoritative for anatomy, turnaround for identity/hair/clothes. Check a complete candidate against both before placing. Do not locally shrink only the head.
3. **Plane group internal proportions were never independently established.** Standing page records plane1982×989 at1.0 and pilot306×1000 at0.666667, with different ground depths. Repair uses an integrated1800×1068 group at0.906801 and describes its internal ratio as authoritative merely because it was generated together. Whole-group scaling cannot change that internal ratio. Night plane1884×940 at0.950455 moves its ground from1331 to1210. Sleeping pilot1049×338 at0.539075 was judged by bent silhouette length instead of head/torso. Plane side-view length≈2.2H is not the same measure as a three-quarter total silhouette width. Measure cockpit and wheels too; encode depth explicitly.
4. **Position and background changes lack a scene transform.** Quiet Prince moves from x1449 to1340 (109px) between s02-p04/p05 without story movement. The sunrise plate is reduced to1801×1345 at[200,149], then the day plate becomes2400×1792. Figures hang below the supporting painted field. A lighting/time change is legitimate; an unrecorded zoom/anchor jump is not. Register horizon, floor and subjects as a coherent scene.
5. **Shadows are generic flattened bottom-silhouette images.** Typical parameters take lower30%, squash0.07, blur9, offset[3,3], opacity ceiling56. A single lowest y cannot support hands, hips and two feet at different depths; the blur can start below the visible source. The repair variant uses three mass regions but still lacks source-edge checks for each contact. Increased opacity does not repair a gap. Build per-bearing contact cores and inspect final pixels.
6. **Emotion and distinct story beats are underspecified in use.** Calm source reuse substitutes for serious requesting, refusal and warm connection. The ram page explicitly says he smiled but reuses the preceding inspecting face. The box delight does exist; it should be preserved rather than described as universally emotionless. c02-s03-p02 already contains an elephant thought, so the chapter does not have zero clouds. The newly requested imagined sheep on s04-p02 was blocked by the older no-cloud plan; that narrow mental-only contract is now revised.
7. **Validation and authority were confused.** File hashes, counts, masks and reconstruction can all pass while the art has these defects. Earlier passing audit records are historical evidence, not authority over this newer review. One repair record also names temporary external reconstruction inputs; use the retained current layer files and migrate provenance inside this project. Retained v002 layer images are cumulative opaque snapshots (alpha255 everywhere), so replay works but their alpha is not an isolated subject/shadow mask; rebuild editable sources from the prepared RGBA and measured transforms. Do not use Chapter 2 v002 as a scale master.
